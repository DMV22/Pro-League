## Decision and limits

The observed technical M2 checks are green with no unexplained schema drift or known Severity 1/2 issue. After this record is reviewed and merged, M2 can be closed and M3 can start. This decision does not approve Production deployment, real federation data, complete Admin workflows, or federation domain acceptance. The latter require their own later evidence and sign-off under the [acceptance blueprint](../architecture/end-to-end-acceptance.md).
