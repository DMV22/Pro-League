# Assign the competition format to the season

Competition Format will belong to each Season rather than being permanently fixed on its Competition. This allows a recurring championship or cup to change its sporting structure between seasons while preserving one continuous Competition identity and the historical rules under which every Season was played; the launch scope supports League and Knockout formats, with hybrid formats deferred.

This decision was superseded when group stages, two-leg ties, optional third-place matches, and hybrid formats became part of the MVP rather than future extensions.
