# Compose a Season format from Competition Stages

Each Season will retain its own Competition Format, but that format will be composed of one or more ordered Competition Stages rather than a single permanent format label. The MVP must support League stages, Group Stages, Knockout stages with one-match or two-leg ties, an optional Third-place Match, and Hybrid Formats because the appropriate sporting structure frequently depends on the number of participating Teams.

## Consequences

A simple league or cup may contain one Competition Stage, while a hybrid Season may advance Teams through several stages with different Stage Formats. Stage configuration and progression rules must be preserved with the Season so historical editions remain interpretable even when the same Competition uses a different structure in another Season. Team count constrains the valid configurations but does not select one automatically: multiple formats may be valid for the same number of Teams, and the Admin selects and configures the format approved by the federation. Any consultation or voting used to reach that decision occurs outside ProLeague; the Portal does not manage representatives, ballots, or votes.

The Admin may start from a Format Template for a league, cup, or group-and-knockout structure and then adjust its groups, rounds, Legs, progression rules, and optional Third-place Match. The resulting Competition Format belongs exclusively to its Season: later changes to the template or another Season cannot alter it.

The federation may change an approved Competition Format after Matches have begun, including adding or removing a Third-place Match. Such a change is recorded as a Format Amendment with a mandatory reason, responsible Admin, effective time, and preserved prior format rather than silently replacing the Season's history.

In a Knockout Format, a dependent Match cannot be scheduled or played until every preceding Knockout Tie that supplies one of its participants is Finalized. Recording a Played Score or an initial Technical Result does not by itself finalize progression while an external federation review remains unresolved, preventing a later ruling from retroactively changing the participants of an already played Match.

Finalization requires an explicit Admin action after the applicable external protest period and any federation review have ended. A deadline or reminder may inform the Admin, but elapsed time never finalizes a Knockout Tie automatically.

The same gate applies between all Competition Stages. After the relevant Knockout Ties, Standings, Standing Adjustments, and Result Rulings are settled, the Admin explicitly marks the current stage as a Finalized Stage; only then are its advancing Teams fixed as participants in the dependent stage.
