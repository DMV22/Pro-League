# Preserve the history of official information

Published official information will not be hard-deleted or silently overwritten. ProLeague will retain the previous value, the responsible Admin, and the time of each correction; a News Article may be archived, a Match Result may be corrected or superseded by a reasoned Technical Result, and an active Competition Format may receive a reasoned Format Amendment. The current applicable version is used publicly while prior values remain in the audit history, providing accountability without presenting obsolete data as current.

Protest submission, deliberation, and approval occur outside ProLeague. After the federation reaches a decision, the Admin records the final Technical Result, its reason, effective time, and any supporting decision document required for publication or audit.

A Technical Result may also establish the official outcome when no Played Score exists, such as a `0:3` decision after a Team fails to appear. The confirmed result makes the Match Finished for progression and Standings without falsely recording that the Match was played.

The Admin enters the exact Technical Result stated in the federation ruling and identifies the affected Teams. ProLeague validates that the score is structurally valid but does not automatically assign `0:3`, `3:0`, or any other value because the applicable ruling may vary by circumstance and regulation.

A later appeal decision is recorded as a new Result Ruling that may replace the current Technical Result or revoke it and restore the Played Score. Result Rulings form an immutable chronological history; only the latest applicable ruling determines the current Match Result, and every change triggers recalculation of affected Standings and unresolved progression.
