# Design System: Pro League - Football of Our Communities

**Project ID:** 9855422714832969954

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

An emotionally engaging Ukrainian community-football portal: cinematic amateur-football photography, expressive headlines, precise match information and a shared-local-identity feeling. NOT an admin dashboard, generic data directory or betting site. This follows the owner's public-page visual references: atmospheric deep emerald surfaces, a bright turquoise brand accent, restrained transparent panels and adult amateur football at sunset. Keep one consistent theme across the page. Do not import any prior test project.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

Deep emerald (#061A17) background; stadium green (#0B2622) panels; soft white (#F4F7F5) primary text; mist (#AEC4BE) supporting text; turquoise (#00E6C4) active links, focus and primary actions; evergreen outline (#245149) boundaries. Use dark text on primary buttons. Photos have intentional dark scrims for readable text, not decorative gradients over all surfaces. Active emphasis may have a restrained local glow; no flashing, automatic ticker or background video. Status meaning must always include text.

## 3. Typography Rules

Geist throughout with full Ukrainian Cyrillic. Hero heading 64/68px, weight 800; section headings 32/40px, weight 700; body 16-18px; supporting text never below 14/20px. Mobile later uses 32-36px hero text. Sentence case except the compact brand wordmark. Full team names wrap naturally. Tabular numerals in scores and standings. No tiny uppercase technical eyebrows or broad tracking.

## 4. Component Stylings

Large photographic story/competition cards: 12px corners; match rows and buttons: 8px. Crisp outlined panels, restrained depth, visible focus and generous touch targets. Photography must be real image content, not gray icon placeholders. Use synthetic illustrative adult football scenes with no recognizable real clubs, sponsors or children; mark as demo imagery. Never imply an AI image documents the fictional match. Do not silently substitute unknown stock rights. Simple original shield-and-football mark beside Pro League; do not copy a real club badge.

## 5. Layout Principles

Desktop reference 1440px, main container 1280px, 80px side margins. Header around 80px. A cinematic hero around 400-440px leads into a compact match strip, with at least its start visible on the first desktop viewport. Lead photographic story plus two supporting articles occupies the main editorial column, with compact standings beside it. Three photo-led competition cards follow; then federation identity and contacts. Create hierarchy, not identical boxes. Mobile is a later separate screen; no claim of responsive acceptance from this desktop.
